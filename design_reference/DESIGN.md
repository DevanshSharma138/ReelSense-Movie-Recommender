## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a private theater atmosphere tailored for cinephiles and curators. It evokes discernment, immersion, and bespoke craftsmanship through deep obsidian foundations, crystalline glass overlays, and luminous champagne accents. The interface avoids aggressive neon tones, clinical utilitarianism, and cold blue undertones in favor of warm, muted charcoal depths and atmospheric gilded illumination.

The aesthetic fuses modern dark-mode minimalism with refined glassmorphism and ambient projection backlighting. Surfaces feel physical yet ethereal, mirroring calibrated OLED displays and premier screening lounges.

## Colors
The palette enforces a strict zero-blue policy to preserve the filmic integrity of cinematic artwork. All dark values derive from pure obsidian and warm charcoal black rather than navy, slate, or cool gray.

### Primary & Accent Palette
- **Primary Accent (`#D4AF37`)**: Authentic metallic champagne gold, reserved for primary CTAs, active media progress bars, curated badges, and active tab indicators.
- **Secondary Accent (`#E5C07B`)**: Soft luminous gold, utilized for ambient highlights, focus rings, and high-tier user status indicators.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#C5A059`)**: Deep antique gold, designated for hover states, subdued metadata highlights, and secondary iconography.
- **Pale Highlight (`#F3E5AB`)**: Candlelight shimmer, applied sparingly to typography inside gold pills and star ratings.

### Neutrals & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#08080A`)**: Ground-zero abyss black, eliminating ambient screen bleed.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#0C0D10`)**: Deep obsidian base for structural containers, master rails, and hero headers.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#16181D`)**: Elevated charcoal for media cards, bottom sheets, and floating panels.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (`#1E2128`)**: Interactive surface for hovered cards, active search rows, and modals.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#2A2E39`)**: Warm hairline borders creating structural precision without visual friction.
- **Text Primary (`#F5F5F7`)**: Pristine warm white for primary movie titles and body copy.
- **Text Secondary (`#9E9EA7`)**: Neutral warm silver for directors, genres, and metadata.
- **Text Muted (`#5F616B`)**: Low-emphasis timestamp and secondary labeling.

## Typography
Typographic scale leverages Outfit for high-impact cinematic display headings and Plus Jakarta Sans for reading comfort and micro-labels. Display treatments use tighter tracking and controlled line heights to evoke high-end film editorial posters, while body copy maintains open horizontal proportions to ensure legibility across varying viewing distances on connected TV, desktop, and mobile displays.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-hybrid grid optimized for continuous horizontal movie rails and responsive vertical feeds. 

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column grid with a fixed 3rem outer margin and 1.5rem gutter. Horizontal carousels bleed edge-to-edge with the first card aligned to the primary layout grid line.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column grid with a 2rem margin and 1.25rem gutter.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4-column grid with a 1.25rem margin and 1rem gutter. Poster grids transition from multi-column rows into 2-column displays or edge-snapping horizontal carousels.

Spacing rhythms rely on vertical multiples of 8px. Density remains spacious around cinema artwork to emphasize visual discovery without clutter.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation abandons cast dropshadows in favor of translucent charcoal tiers, micro-borders, and targeted amber-gold luminescence.

- **Level 0 (Floor)**: Canvas background `#08080A`.
- **Level 1 (Sub-surface)**: Background `#0C0D10` with a 1px border of `#1E2128`. Used for fixed navigation rails and sticky headers.
- **Level 2 (Floating Glass)**: Background `rgba(22, 24, 29, 0.72)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and a micro-border `rgba(42, 46, 57, 0.6)`. Used for movie poster cards, details side-sheets, and filter toolbars.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Active Overlay)**: Background `rgba(30, 33, 40, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)` and a subtle ambient gold perimeter glow: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.
- **Active / Hover State**: Hovered movie cards shift vertically by `-4px`, with border brightening to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)` and an under-card projection halo of `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18), 0 0 0 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`.

## Shapes
Geometry is architectural and streamlined:
- **Poster Cards & Media Containers**: Standardized at `rounded-xl` (1rem / 16px) to frame dynamic video key-art without cutting into essential focal points.
- **Panels, Flyouts, and Modals**: Rendered at `rounded-xl` (1rem / 16px).
- **Interactive Badges, Chips, and Buttons**: Explicit pill-style silhouettes (full radius `9999px`) provide high tactile contrast against sharp 16:9 and 2:3 rectangular movie aspect ratios.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid champagne fill (`#D4AF37`) with obsidian text (`#08080A`), `font-weight: 700`, fully rounded pill radius (`9999px`). On hover, background shifts to `#E5C07B` with an ambient glow of `0 0 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Button**: Translucent obsidian surface (`rgba(22, 24, 29, 0.75)`), 1px micro-border (`#2A2E39`), white text (`#F5F5F7`). On hover, border turns `#D4AF37` and text transitions to `#E5C07B`.
- **Ghost / Icon Button**: Circular (44px × 44px), transparent surface, border `rgba(42, 46, 57, 0.5)`. Gold illumination on press or active state.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curated / Match Score Badge**: Pill shape, background `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`, text `#F3E5AB`, border `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`.
- **Genre & Metadata Chips**: Pill shape, background `rgba(22, 24, 29, 0.6)`, text `#9E9EA7`, border `1px solid #2A2E39`. Hover state shifts border to warm gold and text to `#F5F5F7`.

### Movie Poster Cards
- Proportions strictly follow 2:3 vertical or 16:9 cinematic landscape.
- Corner radius is `16px` (`rounded-xl`), overflow hidden, encapsulated by an internal hairline ring of `1px solid rgba(42, 46, 57, 0.8)`.
- Hover introduces smooth scale (`scale(1.03)`), card elevation shift, border highlight to `#D4AF37`, and ambient underside golden projection blur.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Inputs feature `rgba(12, 13, 16, 0.85)` background fill, border `1px solid #2A2E39`, and placeholder `#5F616B`.
- Focus state eliminates standard browser outlines, applying a crisp border of `#D4AF37` and an inner diffuse ring of `0 0 12px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes utilize a `4px` corner radius; radio controls are circular.
- Inactive state: `rgba(22, 24, 29, 0.9)` fill with `#2A2E39` border.
- Active state: `#D4AF37` solid fill with obsidian `#08080A` checkmark or dot indicator.

### Media Scrubbers & Sliders
- Track background: `#1E2128` (4px height).
- Buffered track: `#2A2E39`.
- Active playback track: Solid `#D4AF37` with an ambient glow filter.
- Scrubber thumb: Circular (14px), solid `#F3E5AB`, with a persistent micro-shadow of `0 0 8px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.6)`.
